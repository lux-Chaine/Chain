## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the commanding precision, discretion, and effortless elegance expected in elite hospitality management. Crafted for portfolio directors, general managers, and front-desk concierges operating multi-property luxury estates, the interface bridges high-density enterprise tooling with the visual poise of a private members' club.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Executive Luxury** with **Subtle Glassmorphism**. Structural framing relies on deep royal navy surfaces paired with crisp architectural hairline dividers, warm champagne gold directional highlights, and atmospheric depth. The emotional resonance is calm, authoritative, and frictionless—providing front-of-house confidence during rapid check-ins alongside analytical clarity for multi-tenant financial reporting.

## Colors

The palette operates primarily in a tailored dark/deep mode to maintain eye comfort during continuous 24/7 operations, while using crisp slate highlights for exceptional contrast across data-dense grids.

### Core Swatches
- **Navy Foundations (Surfaces & Framing):**
  - Surface Darkest: `#0A1128` (Global canvas and Tauri backdrop)
  - Surface Mid: `#101F42` (Primary application sidebars, navigation bars, elevated panels)
  - Surface High: `#1E2E5D` (Card containers, popovers, modal backdrops)
  - Surface Stroke: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` (Sleek champagne glass hairline borders)

- **Champagne Gold Accents:**
  - Base Gold: `#D4AF37` (Key action points, focal badges, active navigation states)
  - Light Gold: `#E5C158` (Hover states, interactive elements, highlights)
  - Pale Shimmer: `#F3E5AB` (Metric callouts, premium tag backgrounds)
  - Deep Burnished Gold: `#997A15` (Border definition, muted structural states)

- **Crisp Slate Neutrals:**
  - Text Primary: `#F8FAFC` (Headings, primary figures, high-contrast matrix data)
  - Text Secondary: `#94A3B8` (Metadata, input labels, column headers)
  - Neutral Accent / Divider: `#1E293B` and `#334155`

- **Operational Hotel Status Colors:**
  - **Occupied:** `#EF4444` / Crimson Tint: `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.14)`
  - **Clean / Available:** `#10B981` / Emerald Tint: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.14)`
  - **Reserved / Incoming:** `#D4AF37` / Gold Tint: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.14)`
  - **Maintenance / Out-of-Order:** `#38BDF8` / Sky Blue Tint: `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.14)`

## Typography

**Hanken Grotesk** serves as the universal typeface across display, editorial headers, body text, and dense tabular matrices. Its razor-sharp geometry, open apertures, and balanced proportion provide flawless horizontal density for timeline reservations and multi-currency guest ledgers.

Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be strictly applied across room numbers, rates, ADR, RevPAR, and folio balances to ensure vertical alignment in ledger calculations. All uppercase subheadings and table headers apply extended tracking (`0.05em` to `0.06em`) to preserve legibility against deep navy container backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system paired with a persistent 72px–260px collapsible tenant navigation drawer. 

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop & Tauri App (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, `margin-desktop` (2rem), `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem). Supports side-by-side room calendar matrices, guest profile slide-overs, and concurrent split folios.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with compact headers, horizontal scroll for room availability tape charts, and condensed room cards.
- **Mobile Handheld (Front-Desk Rover, < 768px):** Single-column stack with persistent bottom-pinned action bars, bottom-sheet guest verification cards, and simplified status lists.

Layout spacing follows a crisp 4px base modular unit, optimizing screen efficiency so reservation managers can oversee multiple floor plans without excessive page scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a layered **Glass & Deep Tone Architecture**, replacing generic drop shadows with luminous champagne rim lighting and dark navy surface elevation:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#0A1128`. Non-interactive background for workspaces and full room grid canvases.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#101F42` with 1px hairline border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`. Subtle drop shadow: `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Slide-Over Drawers):** `#1E2E5D` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Border: 1px `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(5, 10, 24, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Emergency Operational Alerts):** Linear gradient `#1E2E5D` to `#101F42` with top highlight rim: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(243, 229, 171, 0.2)`. Shadow: `0 24px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft Architectural Shape Language** (`roundedness: 1`). Elements feature concise 0.25rem (4px) base corner radii, scaling to 0.5rem (8px) on cards, grids, and modal windows. 

Sharp precision conveys architectural heritage, tailored hospitality standards, and high software density, avoiding toy-like bubbly forms in favor of razor-thin edges, sharp divider rules, and understated rounding.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Luxury Action:** Background in Champagne Gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #B8972E 100%)`) with slate-950 text (`#0A1128`), medium font weight, 4px border radius, and an ambient hover glow (`0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`, `#F8FAFC` text, and subtle hover transition to `#1E2E5D`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Action:** Flat `#F8FAFC` text with gold accent on hover, zero persistent border.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Operational Matrix Chips:** Pill-styled elements featuring subtle background opacity (`0.14`) and solid indicators:
  - *Clean:* Border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, Text `#34D399`, Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.14)`.
  - *Occupied:* Border `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`, Text `#F87171`, Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.14)`.
  - *Reserved:* Border `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`, Text `#E5C158`, Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.14)`.
  - *Maintenance:* Border `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`, Text `#7DD3FC`, Background `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.14)`.

### Room Matrix & Data Grid
- Header cells: `#0A1128` background with uppercase muted gold-slate text (`#94A3B8`, `0.6875rem`).
- Matrix cells: Separated by 1px rules (`#1E293B`). Interactive room blocks display micro room-type badges, assigned housekeeper markers, and instant hover overlays detailing the guest folio.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Solid `#101F42` fill with 1px border (`#1E2E5D`). On focus: Champagne Gold border (`#D4AF37`) with a 2px outer aura ring in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom navy squares with 2px borders in `#D4AF37`. Checked state triggers a gold fill with navy mark check icon.

### Cards & Folio Panels
- Base `#101F42` surface framed in a 1px `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)` border.
- Card headers utilize bottom dividers with a centered 1px subtle gold-fade highlight (`radial-gradient(ellipse at center, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4) 0%, transparent 80%)`).
